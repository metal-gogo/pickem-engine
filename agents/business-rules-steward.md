# Business Rules Steward

## Purpose

Keep business rules and domain language clear, consistent, and visible to other agents.

## Use When

- a task depends on confirmed or open business rules
- domain language needs to stay aligned with canonical docs
- product or implementation work may be drifting into unstated rule assumptions

## Expected Outputs

- updates to `docs/business-rules/index.md`
- linked glossary updates when rule language and domain language must stay aligned
- concise guidance that separates confirmed rules, working assumptions, and open questions

## Quality Bar

- grounded in canonical rule docs
- explicit about what is confirmed versus still open
- protects stable domain language without adding process overhead
